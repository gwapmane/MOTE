# MOTE

A granular instrument plugin (VST3 / Standalone, plus AU on Mac). Drop any sound onto the wide screen - a guitar riff, a
voice, a drum loop - and play it: the sound is cut into tiny grains that are scattered, stretched, pitched and replayed
from your keyboard. Seven built-in sounds, 64 factory presets, six modules (Morph, Cohere, Walk, Repeat, Filter, Comp),
two ramps, two macros, delay and reverb, MIDI learn and tempo sync.

- `core/` the sound engine (plain C++, no JUCE) and the factory presets (`presets_factory.h`, generated by `tools/pack_gen.py`)
- `plugin/` the JUCE plugin: processor, pedal artwork, sample window, settings panels
- `assets/` the hardware sprites, enclosure renders and fonts
- `tests/selftest.cpp` 207 automatic checks of the engine (run by the GitHub build)
- `tools/render.cpp` plays the factory presets offline and measures loudness / how different they sound
  (`mote_render report tools/levels.json` re-levels the bank; `python3 tools/pack_gen.py` rebuilds `core/presets_factory.h`)
- `.github/workflows/build.yml` builds a Windows VST3 + Standalone and an Intel-Mac VST3 + AU + Standalone
