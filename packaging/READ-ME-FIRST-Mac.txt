MOTE  v0.1  -  Intel Mac: VST3 + AU + Standalone      (a granular instrument: drop any sound in, play it)

QUICK START (FL Studio on your Mac)
1. Unzip the download.
2. Double-click INSTALL.command.  If macOS says it can't be opened: right-click it > Open > Open.
   (It copies MOTE into  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3  and  .../Components  and clears the "downloaded" mark.)
3. FL Studio > Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins.
4. MOTE is an INSTRUMENT: look under Installed > Generators.

To just try it, right-click MOTE.app > Open (the first time), and click the keys on the screen.

If macOS still refuses to load it: System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway".

HOW YOU USE IT
1. DRAG ANY SOUND onto the wide screen (wav, aiff, flac, ogg, mp3 - a guitar riff, a vocal, a drum loop, anything).
   Or click the screen and choose a file. Up to 40 seconds is kept, and it is saved inside your FL project.
2. Play notes. The sound is cut into tiny pieces ("grains") and replayed as an instrument.
3. Drag across the screen to move POSITION - that is where in your sound the grains are taken from.
   The soft blobs you see are the grains being fired as you play.
Seven built-in sounds are in the SAMPLE knob if you just want to hear it work (the last position is YOUR sample).

THE BIG KNOBS (top row)
 POSITION  where in the sound the grains come from      SCATTER  how far from that spot they wander
 DENSITY   how many grains per second                   SIZE     how long each grain is (small = grainy, big = smooth)
 PITCH     whole sound up / down in steps               JITTER   random pitch wobble per grain
 SPREAD    stereo width                                 SPEED    how fast the position travels (TRAVEL / BOUNCE)
 MAGNET    pulls grains toward the hits in your sound (drums, plucks)
THE SIX MODULES (the levers with a small knob): switch on what you want
 MORPH  blend in a second sound (BLEND; drop the second sound on the right half of the screen)
 COHERE locks the grains to the note you play, so it sounds like a clean synth (LOCK)
 WALK   steps through slices of your sound in time with your DAW (PATTERN)
 REPEAT stutters what you play, beat-repeat style (LENGTH)
 FILTER sweeps tone (CUTOFF)                       COMP squashes it so it sounds thick (SQUASH)
THE SMALL KNOBS (bottom): ATTACK DECAY SUSTAIN RELEASE (how a note starts and ends), KEY + SCALE (make grains land on
 notes that sound good together - pick Pentatonic and you can't hit a wrong note), VOICES, LEVEL, DELAY, REVERB.
PLAY lever: HOLD (stays at POSITION), TRAVEL (slides along your sound), BOUNCE (slides back and forth).
A / LIVE / B lever: two memory slots for quick sound changes.     Left footswitch = on/off.  Right footswitch = LATCH
 (keeps the notes playing; hold it for momentary).

IN THE PLUGIN
- Bar on top: < preset name > (click the name: 64 factory presets in groups, plus My Presets), Save, S/M/L window size,
  the up-arrow hides the bar so only the pedal shows, gear = settings.
- Settings > LOOK: body colour, lights colour, texture, your own picture, your own video / GIF / picture in the window.
- Settings > SOUND: load a sample (and a second one for MORPH), grain shape, reverse, filter type, walk / repeat details,
  delay + reverb details, two Ramps (knobs move by themselves) and two Macros (one knob moves several), tempo, MIDI learn.
- MIDI knobs: right-click any knob on the pedal, then move a knob on your controller.
- Piano keys on the screen play it too (click and hold).
